About Us

O.B. Montessori Center with Operation Brotherhood Community Foundation (OBCF) lives out its legacy of service through its outreaches and social responsibility programs, affirming that education is not an end in itself. With a vision to empower children and communities, we strive to create a world where every individual has the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Instead, it is the beginning of changing society and the nation for the better. Since 1966, OBMC has been raising and developing generations of global citizens committed to service and the cause of peace.

Advancing the Montessori Way

At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, we are deeply committed to the philosophy and educational method developed by Dr. Maria Montessori. Due to such holistic method, each child is nurtured.

We believe that every child is unique and has the potential to thrive in a supportive and stimulating environment. Our dedicated educators are trained in the Montessori method, ensuring that each child receives personalized attention and guidance.

We are proud to be part of a global network of Montessori schools, sharing best practices and continuously improving our approach to education. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in the achievements of our students and the positive impact they have on their communities.
